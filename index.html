Frequently asked

Good questions deserve clear answers.

01Who do you work with?

We support startups, SMEs, family businesses and established organisations that need clearer strategy, stronger systems or a practical growth roadmap.

02How does an engagement begin?

It starts with a discovery conversation to define the challenge, desired outcome, scope, timing and the right consulting approach.

03Can you support implementation?

Yes. Support can extend into action planning, process design, team enablement and progress reviews.

04Do you provide legal and tax advice?

Legal and tax advice should be delivered by qualified professionals. Prime can coordinate relevant partners when needed.

05How are consulting fees calculated?

Fees depend on scope, complexity, duration and implementation support. A clear proposal is provided before work begins.

06Do you provide online consultations?

Yes. Consultations can be delivered online, in person or as a hybrid engagement.

Building a scalable sales growth system

Serendib Retail Co. · Retail & Distribution · 12 weeks · Fictional demonstration

Challenge

Low sales conversion, inconsistent follow-up and unclear customer segments across three outlets.

Our approach

Redesigned customer segments, the sales pipeline, team responsibilities and the weekly performance review rhythm.

Outcome

A clearer lead-management process, stronger ownership and a sales system designed to scale across new locations.

Repositioning a local hospitality brand

Ceylon Bay Hotels · Hospitality & Tourism · 10 weeks · Fictional demonstration

Challenge

A strong guest experience was not translating into a distinctive market position or consistent direct bookings.

Our approach

Clarified the brand promise, target guest profiles, content direction and direct-booking customer journey.

Outcome

A focused brand story, a more consistent digital presence and a practical roadmap for growing direct demand.

Turning daily operations into a clear system

Lankora Manufacturing · Manufacturing · 16 weeks · Fictional demonstration

Challenge

Production delays, unclear handovers and limited visibility across purchasing, inventory and delivery.

Our approach

Mapped core workflows, introduced operating procedures, role ownership and a management dashboard structure.

Outcome

More consistent handovers, clearer operational accountability and improved visibility for management decisions.
